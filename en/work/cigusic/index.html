Cigusic

Product concept

Every brand has its own playlist.

Cigusic is an electric cigarette holder that detects the brand of cigarette being smoked and plays a matching playlist through a built-in speaker. Each brand triggers its own set of songs, for a smoking experience that feels personal: technology and style, with a musical twist on a classic habit.
