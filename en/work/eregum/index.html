Eregum

Brand identity and packaging

A chewing gum brand, invented from scratch.

A fictional brand built from nothing: name, concept, logo, packaging and promotional material. Of several directions, the most provocative one won: EREGUM, a gum designed to improve sexual performance, directly inspired by Viagra. That one idea gave every design decision a clear direction.
