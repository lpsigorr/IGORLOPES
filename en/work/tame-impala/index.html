Tame Impala Experience

Interactive motion

A billboard that looks back.

A vertical billboard for a live show, made to be played with as you walk past. A camera follows your hands: the eye on screen follows them, and depending on the hand sign, text appears and the visuals intensify until the concert details are there: Tame Impala, 31/08, Parc du Cinquantenaire, 21h. The eye and the trip are a nod to psychedelic rock. Built in TouchDesigner, with MediaPipe for the hand tracking.
