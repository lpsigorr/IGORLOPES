Silo

Brand identity and product concept

A quiet room with good light.

A desktop workspace for writers, researchers and founders who spend hours in dense PDFs, articles and notes, and turn them into clear writing. Most tools are either sterile spreadsheets or noisy AI workspaces full of notifications. Silo strips the clutter away, so it feels closer to a wooden desk with heavy paper and a sharp pencil.
