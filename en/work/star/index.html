For Soudesune, a YF Agency label

  • The brief

    YF Agency wanted a new collection for Soudesune, its streetwear label. They had seen Igor's sketchbook and wanted those drawings on clothes.

  • What Igor made

    The creative direction, a hand-drawn typeface, the logos, the collection in 2D and 3D, a brand film and the campaign visuals.

  • The result

    A complete collection, from the first sketch to the campaign, in four months.

STAR isn't just clothing, it's direction. A symbol for those building in silence and shining on purpose.
