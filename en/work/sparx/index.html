Final-year project, Erasmushogeschool Brussel

  • The brief

    Final-year project: find a problem worth solving in your own field. For Igor that was boxing, and the lockdown months spent training alone, with no coach to say if a punch landed.

  • What Igor made

    A sensor sleeve for any punching bag, the electronics inside it, the app that reads every punch, and the brand around it.

  • The result

    A working first prototype that logs every hit by zone, tested on a real bag.

  • See where you hit

    Every punch is logged on the zone it lands on.

  • Live count

    Punches and pace show up while you train.

  • Rounds that run themselves

    Timer and sound cues, your eyes stay on the bag.

  • No subscription

    Everything runs locally, on the phone.

The app: black and white, big words, readable mid-round.
