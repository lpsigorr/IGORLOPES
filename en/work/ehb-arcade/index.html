EHB Arcade

Cabinet, electronics and gameBrussels

One machine: wood, wires and a game.

A full arcade machine that brings craft, electronics and software together, with a game made for it. The joystick and buttons answer the game the way a classic cabinet should.

Credits

  • Electronics: joystick and buttons

  • The brief

    Every student built an arcade machine. The best one would stand at the school fair to draw in future students.

  • What Igor made

    The cabinet, the electronics and the game made for it.

  • The result

    Igor's machine was selected for the fair, then moved to the school hallway, where students could play it any time.

A working machine, ready to play.
