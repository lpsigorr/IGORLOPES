Mordida Records

Brand concept and identity system

A record shop for hot sauce.

Mordida, Spanish for bite, is a small-batch brand of fermented hot sauce and roasted chili crisp. It turns away from macho novelty sauces and sterile wellness jars alike, and borrows the ritual, the liner-note detail and the collectible energy of a vintage record shop instead. Every flavour is a release, and every jar is made to stay out on the table and get passed around.
