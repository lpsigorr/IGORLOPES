World Without Bees

Installation and interactionBrussels

Take the bees away, and the world goes grey.

Bees pollinate around 70 of the 100 crop species that feed 90% of the world. Remove the bees from a cardboard hive and the landscape on screen empties and turns grey. Put them back and it comes alive again.
