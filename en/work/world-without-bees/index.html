World Without Bees

Installation and interactionBrussels

Take the bees away, and the world goes grey.

Bees pollinate around 70 of the 100 crop species that feed 90% of the world. Remove the bees from the hive and the landscape on screen empties and turns grey. Put them back and it comes alive again. Each bee carries a magnet: an Arduino reads which bees are home and sends the values to TouchDesigner, where the landscape is generated live.

  • The brief

    Use an interactive installation to make people aware of a global issue.

  • What Igor made

    A hive with magnetic bees, the electronics that read them, and a landscape generated live in TouchDesigner.

  • The result

    A message you understand in one gesture: take the bees away, and the world on screen dies.
